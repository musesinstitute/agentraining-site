Capability Structure · V1

Second-level capabilities define the training skeleton. Individual scenarios will be attached only after this structure is stable.

1. Patient & Family Service Management

Service Communication · expectations, listening, explanation
Complaint Handling · acknowledge, clarify, respond
Difficult Conversations · emotion, boundaries, trust
Service Recovery & Follow-up · ownership, handoff, closure

2. Staff & Team Management

Performance Coaching · feedback, expectations, improvement
Staffing & Scheduling Conversations · constraints, fairness, communication
Onboarding & Training · standards, role clarity, reinforcement
Retention & Team Communication · burnout signals, conflict, engagement

3. Conflict & Escalation Management

De-escalation · emotion and immediate communication
Issue Discovery · facts, needs, root cause
Escalation Judgment · recognize authority and safety boundaries
Resolution & Next Action · ownership, action, communication

4. Compliance & Policy Communication

Privacy Communication · explain and protect information appropriately
Policy Explanation · communicate approved organizational rules
Boundary Recognition · know when not to improvise
Escalation to Authorized Roles · clinician, privacy/compliance, or leadership as appropriate

5. Operations & Follow-up Management

Operational Handoffs · clear ownership and transfer
Incident Follow-up · document action and close the loop
Service Workflow Communication · delays, coordination, expectations
Manager Follow-through · assignment, coaching, evidence, re-practice

MVP boundary: This industry pack begins with management and communication training. Clinical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, real patient records/PHI, EHR integrations, and claims of legal or HIPAA certification are outside the initial training scope.
