Why not just ChatGPT?

ChatGPT answers an individual question. AgentTraining.ai keeps a record across sessions and over time: company-specific knowledge → assigned practice → AI evaluation → real case follow-through → manager visibility. The difference is not that it can analyse a conversation — it is that every assessment keeps its evidence, so you can check it, disagree with it, and see it revised. At 30/60-day reviews, the system revisits earlier judgements against new evidence, manager overrides and outcomes so the next decision can change.
Long-form comparison is not part of this demo.

The supervisor takeaway

You do not need to listen to every call. The system points at where help may be needed and shows you what that judgement rests on — then you decide whether to act on it. The final call stays yours, and your reasons are kept with it.
