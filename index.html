Four Studios. One Goal.

Turn new recruits into capable advisors.把新人真正帶出來。

Practice Studio

Practice before the client opportunity is on the line.在見客戶以前，把問題練出來。

AI plays a realistic client so advisors can rehearse needs analysis, objection handling, follow-up, and closing, again and again.用 AI 模擬真實客戶，反覆練需求分析、異議處理、跟進與成交。

Mistakes you can make in practice shouldn't be made in front of a client.能在練習裡犯的錯，就不要留到客戶面前再犯。

Client Studio

Turn real client challenges into the next practice session.把真實業務的問題帶回訓練。

"It's too expensive." A client who won't decide. A follow-up that goes nowhere. A case that's stuck. Each real difficulty becomes targeted practice.客戶說太貴、遲遲不決定、Follow-up 沒結果、Case 卡住——把真實困難變成下一次針對性訓練。

Knowledge Studio

Turn company knowledge into skills advisors can actually use.把公司的知識變成真正會用的能力。

Product, underwriting, and sales materials don't just sit in a folder. They flow into practice, so advisors learn to explain them and use them.產品、核保和銷售資料不只是存在資料夾裡，而是進入練習，讓新人逐漸能夠說出來、用出來。

Manager Studio

See who needs help before they quietly disengage.在人離開以前，看見他正在掉隊。

See who is practicing, who is improving, who has stopped, and who keeps getting stuck, then assign the next practice in time.看見誰在練、誰在進步、誰開始停下來、誰反覆卡住，並及時安排下一步訓練。
