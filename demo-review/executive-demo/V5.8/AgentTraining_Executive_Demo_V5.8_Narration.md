# AgentTraining.ai Executive Demo V5.8 — 3-Minute Manager Impact Cut — Narration

**Status:** draft narration for review; not approved, not recorded. US English, target pace 110–135 wpm.
**Length:** six scenes, 180 seconds (3:00). Subtitle timings in the SRT are generated from the HTML player’s own cue allocator.
**Value proposition:** Turn your team’s knowledge into AI practice — and practice into stronger agents.
**Roles:** You = team manager. David = trainee agent (learner). Michael = fictional potential life insurance client, played by the AI in simulated practice. AI Coach = feedback tool.

## Scene 1 — The Problem (0:00–0:20)

Your agents finish courses and pass tests.

But are they ready for the conversations that matter?

Usually, you find out only when a real client is on the line.

AgentTraining.ai turns your team’s knowledge into AI practice — and practice into stronger agents.

*On screen: Course completions and test results do not show how an agent handles a client conversation. — 42 words / 20 s ≈ 126 wpm.*

## Scene 2 — Knowledge → AI Intelligence (0:20–0:52)

Start with your approved company materials.

AI pulls out the key points and links each one back to its source.

It flags which topics look most important — as suggestions for you to review.

Then it creates a question bank and different practice scenarios: different clients, objections, and products.

You review what it creates before your team uses it.

*On screen: Approved materials → source-linked key points → question bank and practice scenarios → your review. — 58 words / 32 s ≈ 109 wpm.*

## Scene 3 — AI Client Simulation (0:52–1:25)

Now David, a new agent on your team, practices with an AI client.

The AI plays Michael, a fictional potential client.

Michael says: “I’m not sure we need more life insurance.”

David answers in the practice chat.

“Our term life policy is affordable, and it protects your family for twenty years.”

It is practice, not a live client call — so a weak answer costs nothing.

*On screen: David, a trainee agent, practices in a text chat with an AI playing Michael, a fictional potential client. — 65 words / 33 s ≈ 118 wpm.*

## Scene 4 — Personal AI Coach (1:25–1:57)

Then the AI Coach looks at what David actually said.

“You explained the product before asking about Michael’s needs.”

Why it matters: Michael has not said what worries him yet.

A better way to say it: “That’s fair. What makes you feel your current coverage may be enough?”

David tries again, with the same AI client.

Specific feedback, right after each answer.

*On screen: Specific feedback, why it matters, a better way to say it — then a retry with the same AI client. — 62 words / 32 s ≈ 116 wpm.*

## Scene 5 — Manager · Team Growth (1:57–2:26)

You, the manager, can review the practice transcript and the coaching notes.

Empathy, knowledge, next step — the evidence is in David’s own words.

Then you choose his next practice.

Practice, Client, Knowledge, and Leadership studios are designed to work together in one Team Growth Hub.

The AI recommends. You decide.

*On screen: Review the practice evidence, choose the next assignment, and see how the studios fit together. — 50 words / 29 s ≈ 103 wpm.*

## Scene 6 — Outcome · Next Step (2:26–3:00)

Knowledge becomes practice.

Practice becomes coaching.

Coaching builds better preparation, and your team keeps developing.

Your materials. AI practice. Personal coaching. Your decisions.

See how your agents prepare — before the conversations that matter.

Start with one team and two important client conversations.

Explore how it works.

Or request a fourteen-day private pilot for your team.

*On screen: Explore how it works, or request a 14-day private pilot (by request; subject to confirmation). — 55 words / 34 s ≈ 97 wpm.*

## Production truth guards

- Simulated practice only. No real client call is recorded, heard, or monitored.
- Voice input in the product today is speech-to-text dictation into a text chat; the AI replies in text. Spoken two-way practice and live coaching modes are **VISION / IN DEVELOPMENT — NOT YET AVAILABLE**, and are tagged that way on screen and on the endcard.
- Source-linked key points, question bank and practice scenario generation exist in code with manager review; the full document-to-scenario route still needs end-to-end live UAT.
- “Flags which topics look most important” refers to the knowledge map’s core / supporting flag; it is a reviewable suggestion, not a validated relevance score or a prediction of team growth.
- Coaching wording (feedback / why it matters / better way to say it) is an authored example, not verified production output.
- Team Growth Hub is a concept view; the four studios’ naming is not final (the live studios page currently lists Manager Studio, not Leadership Studio).
- Explore How It Works has no verified destination yet. The 14-day private pilot is by request, subject to confirmation.
- No promised retention, recruiting, sales, booked appointments, or automatic manager actions.
- Final recorded voiceover must be timed and the SRT re-checked against it.
