# AgentTraining.ai Executive Demo V5.9.1 — Manager Impact / High-Value Case

**Status:** Draft for executive review; not approved, not recorded or published.
**Runtime:** 3:20 (200 seconds), six scenes. Approximate timing only until a real voiceover is recorded.
**Purpose:** Show managers that AgentTraining.ai prepares agents for both ordinary family protection and potentially multi-million-dollar, evidence-based business protection conversations.
**Roles:** David = trainee insurance agent; Michael = fictional ordinary client; Richard = fictional successful business owner whose company is valued at $12M. All conversations and coaching are authored illustrations.

**Important:** This is not a $12M insurance need; $12M is a fictional business valuation. A high coverage amount is never presumed, recommended or promised.

## Scene 1 — The Problem (0:00–0:20)

Your agents finish courses and pass tests.

But are they ready for the conversations that matter?

Usually, you find out only when a real client is on the line.

AgentTraining.ai turns your team’s knowledge into AI practice — and practice into stronger agents.

## Scene 2 — Knowledge → AI Intelligence (0:20–0:47)

Start with your approved company materials.

AI pulls out the key points and links each one back to its source.

It flags which topics look most important — as suggestions for you to review.

Then it creates questions and practice scenarios — from everyday families to high-net-worth business owners.

You review it all before your team uses it.

*Narration: 58 words / 27 s ≈ 129 wpm.*

## Scene 3 — AI Client Simulation (0:47–1:45)

David practices two client conversations.

Michael: “I’m not sure we need more life insurance.”

David pitches a product too soon.

Richard: “I already have an insurance agent. Why should I talk to you?”

David: “I respect that relationship. I’m not asking you to switch advisors.”

“Would you consider a second opinion on your family and business protection?”

Richard: “My business is worth twelve million dollars now, nearly twice what it was three years ago.”

“What would you check that my agent hasn’t already covered?”

David: “Whether your buy-sell funding and key-person protection still fit — ideally with your advisor involved.”

The goal isn’t an instant sale. It’s earning the right to a meaningful review.

*Director note: On screen: Michael everyday objection → short product-first answer → Richard, fictional business owner with a $12M company, reports business nearly doubled in value since the prior review. David asks whether buy-sell funding and key-person protection still fit. This is a training simulation, not a real client or verified policy gap.*
*Narration: 114 words / 58 s ≈ 118 wpm.*

## Scene 4 — Personal AI Coach (1:45–2:28)

The AI Coach reviews what David actually said.

With Michael, ask about needs before pitching products.

With Richard, respect the advisor — then examine what changed as the company grew.

Could buy-sell funding or key-person protection now be insufficient?

Evaluate existing policies and financial obligations before recommending changes.

If a documented shortfall exists, the discussion might involve multi-million-dollar coverage.

But that is a question to investigate, never a guaranteed sale.

Respect, discover, validate, earn the next conversation.

*Director note: On screen: AI Coach contrasts Michael’s needs-first basics with Richard’s sophisticated discussion. A large potential policy is conditional on real obligations, existing coverage, appropriate expertise and documented need; no size is promised.*
*Narration: 77 words / 43 s ≈ 107 wpm.*

## Scene 5 — Manager · Team Growth (2:28–2:55)

As manager, you review both practice transcripts and coach notes.

Michael builds fundamentals. Richard prepares agents for complex, high-value conversations.

A documented need could lead to a multi-million-dollar policy discussion — not a promised sale.

You choose the next practice. AI recommends. You decide.

*Director note: On screen: illustrative manager transcript and practice assignments for foundation and advanced skills; Team Growth Hub is a concept.*

Knowledge becomes practice.

Practice becomes coaching.

Coaching builds better preparation, and your team keeps developing.

Your materials. AI practice. Personal coaching. Your decisions.

Start with one team and two important client conversations.

Explore how it works.

Or request a fourteen-day private pilot for your team.

## Production and compliance boundaries

- Both clients, their businesses, financial figures and dialogue are fictional; all AI Coach examples are authored and illustrative.
- The business valuation of $12M is not an insurance coverage amount. Existing policies and buy-sell obligations are unknown. No identified shortfall, guaranteed multi-million-dollar case, conversion rate or commission is implied.
- A business valuation change is a reason to ask questions, not a substitute for needs analysis. Any recommendations require suitable advice, applicable licensing, underwriting, and professional legal/tax review where appropriate.
- No real client calls are recorded or monitored. Current speech input is dictation-to-text; spoken two-way practice and live voice coaching are not available.
- Knowledge-to-scenario flow still needs end-to-end UAT; team-level skills, manager assignment labels and hub are illustrative concepts.
- The "Explore How It Works" control has no verified destination and remains a labeled prototype, not a clickable working link. Private pilot is by request and subject to confirmation.
- This file and the HTML preview are not a recorded voiceover or MP4; the SRT is synchronized to the programmed timeline, not to a final recording.
