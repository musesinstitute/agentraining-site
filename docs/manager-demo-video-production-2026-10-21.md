# AgentTraining.ai — Manager Demo Video Production Plan
Date: 2026-10-21
Status: Draft production brief; video not yet recorded
Source: current merged manager-demo-review.html → opening → hub → phases → mission-control-v2-1.html

## Goal
Produce a concise English-language 90-second manager-facing video that motivates an insurance team leader to request a 14-day pilot. Audience: team managers, agency leaders, training directors. No fabricated results, testimonials, or implemented capabilities.

## 90-second storyboard / voiceover
| Time | Screen capture | English narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–12s | Opening: manager risk briefing, headline | "You lead a team. But can you see who needs coaching before a small problem becomes a missed opportunity?" |
| 12–28s | Hub: show all four Studios; select each in turn | "AgentTraining.ai brings Practice, Client, Knowledge, and Leadership into one connected team growth and retention hub." |
| 28–43s | Practice / Client entry points (show only working flows) | "Advisors practice real client conversations. Managers can use those learning signals to guide coaching decisions." |
| 43–58s | Three Phases / Seven Steps | "From spotting a coaching need to choosing an action and reviewing progress, the manager has a clearer workflow." |
| 58–76s | Mission Control V2.2: simulated team decisions, time budget, evidence | "Explore a five-advisor simulation. Compare priorities, allocate limited coaching time, and ask what evidence would change your decision." |
| 76–90s | End slate: AgentTraining.ai / 14-day pilot | "Train every advisor. Coach every manager. Grow every team. Ask about a 14-day pilot." |

## Capture sequence
1. Start at https://agentraining.ai/manager-demo-review.html .
2. Record clean desktop capture at 1920×1080, browser zoom 100%, no personal accounts, email addresses, or client data visible.
3. Capture opening, Hub (click all four Studios), Phases, then existing Mission Control V2.2. Use staged simulated data only.
4. Confirm every page loads and buttons work on the production site before filming. Re-record any transition that shows a broken link.
5. Add captions, company logo, short CTA and optional background music with licensed rights.
6. Export 1920×1080 MP4 (H.264), plus a captioned 60-second cut if time permits.

## Claims / compliance checklist
- Explicitly label simulation screens as demonstration data.
- Do not imply planned privacy protections or integrations are already operational.
- Do not claim verified ROI, sales increases, retention gains, or pilot outcomes.
- Distinguish manager decision support from automatic real assignments.
- Use the existing V2.2, never the deprecated five-learners V2.
- Confirm pilot enrollment/contact destination before placing a clickable CTA.
