## One-sentence differentiation

## Recommended 14-day pilot shape (proposal; confirm operational feasibility)
Audience: one team manager and 3–5 advisors.
Day 0: manager kickoff; choose 1–2 real coaching priorities; invite advisors.
Days 1–3: baseline practice using approved scenarios; record the evidence that is actually available.
Days 4–10: repeat targeted practice; manager reviews what is visible and makes coaching decisions.
Days 11–14: compare baseline vs follow-up *practice* evidence, gather manager/advisor feedback, decide whether to continue.
Do not claim improved client conversions or retention from a 14-day pilot without independent evidence.

## Pilot success criteria
1. Access: invited participants can enter and resume as expected on supported devices.
2. Practice: advisors can complete a scenario and access feedback.
3. Manager: manager can inspect permitted results and explain the next coaching decision.
4. Trust: participants understand what is simulated, stored, visible to managers, and not yet implemented.
5. Commercial signal: manager requests a follow-up or paid-team proposal.
If a feature is not operational, label it and collect feedback rather than pretending it worked.
